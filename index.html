Features

  • Desktop sidebar for your everyday tools — notes, checklist, calendar and calculator in one dock
  • Auto-start — Side Dock opens with your computer, ready when you are
  • Built-in Habit Tracker to build daily streaks
  • Light and Dark themes — one settings button at the top switches everything
  • Stays out of the way — the dock slides in only when you need a tool
  • Free to download and use, no account needed
