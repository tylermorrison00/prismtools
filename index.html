Features

  • Desktop sidebar for your everyday tools — notes, checklist, calendar and calculator in one dock
  • Auto-start — Side Dock opens with your computer, ready when you are
  • Built-in Habit Tracker to build daily streaks
  • Light and Dark themes — one settings button at the top switches everything
  • Stays out of the way — the dock slides in only when you need a tool
  • Free to download and use, no account needed

Zenith

Your discipline app — habit tracker, tasks, notes, calculator and more focus tools in one place. The same app on laptop and mobile, with your data shared across both, and an optional edge sidebar on desktop for the tools you pick.

Download for Windows
Full setup installer (.exe) — click Download and the setup file downloads directly. Open it, install, and Zenith is ready. No Node.js, no extra software, nothing else to set up.

Features

  • Habit Tracker, Tasks, Notes, Calculator and more discipline tools in one app
  • Same app on laptop and mobile — your data stays the same on both
  • Optional edge sidebar on desktop — you choose which tools show there
  • Five theme colours — amber, green, yellow, blue and purple
  • Updates from inside the app — one click on Update app, no website visit needed
  • Simple account — create an account with your email and password, then log in with the same details
